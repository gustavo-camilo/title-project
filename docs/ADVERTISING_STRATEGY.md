# Advertising Strategy

| Channel | Purpose | Budget Allocation | Priority |
|---------|---------|-------------------|----------|
| Google Ads (Search) | Capture high-intent buyers searching for title research | 40% | Primary |

## Google Ads Strategy

### Campaign 1: High-Intent Search (English)

**Objective**: Capture users actively searching for title search services.

**Keywords** (exact + phrase match):
- "title search for auction property"
- "property lien search service"
- "title search service online"
- "real estate title search"
- "tax deed title search"
- "foreclosure title search"
- "check liens on property"
- "property due diligence service"

**Negative keywords**:
- "free" (we're a paid service)
- "job" / "career" (not hiring ads)
- "title insurance" (different service)
- "auto" / "car" / "vehicle" (different industry)

**Ad copy examples**:

Ad 1:
```
Headline: Auction Title Search | 24-48 Hour Reports
Description: Stop guessing at auction. Get a full title report
covering liens, judgments & encumbrances. From $30/property.
```

Ad 2:
```
Headline: Hidden Liens? Find Them Before You Bid
Description: Comprehensive title research for tax deed &
foreclosure properties. Used by 500+ investors nationwide.
```

**Landing page**: Dedicated LP with headline matching ad, pricing, CTA to sign up.

**Bid strategy**: Start with Manual CPC, switch to Target CPA after 30 conversions.

**Budget**: $30-50/day to start, scale based on CPA.

## Facebook & Instagram Ads Strategy

### Campaign 1: Awareness (English)

**Objective**: Introduce TitleSearch Pro to auction investors who aren't actively searching.

**Audience targeting**:
- Interest: Real estate investing, property flipping, foreclosures, tax liens
- Behavior: Engaged shoppers, small business owners
- Demographics: 25-55, US-based
- Lookalike: 1% lookalike from customer email list (once available)

**Ad formats**:
- **Carousel**: Show the 8 types of liens we check (one card per lien type)
- **Video**: 30-60 sec "Before TitleSearch Pro vs. After" showing scattered research vs. clean report
- **Single image**: Sample report preview with headline "What's hiding behind that auction property?"

**Budget**: $20-40/day.

### Campaign 2: Brazilian Investor Targeting (Portuguese)

**Objective**: Reach Brazilian investors interested in US real estate.

**Audience targeting**:
- Language: Portuguese (Brazil)
- Location: United States (Brazilian diaspora) + Brazil (planning to invest in US)
- Interest: Real estate investing, "leilao de imoveis", US immigration, O Cacador de Titulos
- Custom audience: Visitors to Portuguese landing page

**Ad copy** (Portuguese):
```
Investindo em imoveis de leilao nos EUA?

Descubra hipotecas ocultas, onus fiscais e gravames ANTES
de dar seu lance.

TitleSearch Pro - O unico servico de pesquisa de titulos
bilingue para investidores brasileiros.

A partir de $30 por imovel.
```

**Budget**: $15-25/day.

### Campaign 3: Retargeting

**Objective**: Convert website visitors who didn't sign up or purchase.

**Audience**:
- Website visitors (last 30 days) who didn't sign up
- Signed up but didn't purchase (last 14 days)
- Purchased but haven't ordered in 30+ days

**Ad content**:
- Visitor → signup: Testimonials, sample report, "Your first search starts at $49"
- Signup → purchase: "Your account is ready. Get your first title report today."
- Inactive → re-engage: "New properties hitting auction this week. Don't bid blind."

**Budget**: $10-15/day.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **CPA** (Cost Per Acquisition) | Ad spend / new paying customers | < $30 |
| **ROAS** (Return On Ad Spend) | Revenue / ad spend | > 3x |
| **CTR** (Click-Through Rate) | Clicks / impressions | > 2% (search), > 0.5% (social) |
| **Conversion Rate** | Signups / clicks | > 5% |
| **LTV:CAC** | Lifetime value / customer acquisition cost | > 3:1 |

## A/B Testing Plan

### Landing Pages
- Test headline variations (fear-based vs. benefit-based)
- Test pricing display (monthly vs. per-credit emphasis)
- Test CTA button text ("Start Your Search" vs. "Get Your Report" vs. "Protect Your Investment")
- Test English vs. Portuguese default for Brazilian-targeted ads

### Ad Creatives
- Test image vs. video ads on Facebook/Instagram
- Test different lien types in headlines (IRS liens vs. HOA liens vs. general)
- Test urgency messaging ("Before your next auction" vs. evergreen)
- Test testimonial-based ads vs. feature-based ads

### Audiences
- Test interest-based vs. lookalike audiences
- Test broad vs. narrow targeting
- Test demographic segments (age, income level)

## Tracking & Attribution

- **Google Analytics 4**: Website traffic, conversion events
- **Facebook Pixel**: Retargeting, conversion tracking
- **Google Tag Manager**: Central tag management
- **UTM parameters**: On all ad links for campaign attribution
- **Stripe metadata**: Link purchases to ad campaigns for true ROAS
- **PostHog or Mixpanel**: In-app funnel analysis (signup → purchase → order → repeat)

## Compliance

- All ads comply with platform advertising policies
- No misleading claims about guaranteed results
- Clear disclaimer that reports are informational, not legal advice
- Privacy-compliant tracking (cookie consent banner, CCPA compliance)
- Ad accounts use business verification for trust
