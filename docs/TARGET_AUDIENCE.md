# Target Audience & Market Analysis

## Primary Market

Real estate investors who purchase properties at **tax deed auctions** and **foreclosure auctions** in the United States. These buyers need fast, reliable title research to make informed bidding decisions under time pressure.

## Market Size

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

- **US foreclosure auctions**: ~200,000 properties/year (varies with economic cycle)
- **US tax deed/tax lien auctions**: ~300,000+ properties/year across all counties
- **Total auction properties**: ~500,000/year
- **Average research per property**: Assume 30% of auction participants conduct paid research
- **TAM**: ~150,000 paid title searches/year x $40 avg = **$6M/year**

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

- **Online auction buyers** (most tech-savvy, most likely to use a platform): ~40% of TAM
- **English + Portuguese speakers**: Covers ~95% of US auction market plus Brazilian investors
- **SAM**: ~60,000 paid searches/year = **$2.4M/year**

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — Year 1

- **Target**: Capture 5-10% of SAM in first year
- **SOM**: 3,000-6,000 paid searches/year = **$120K-$240K/year**
- **Monthly**: 250-500 credits/month

## Pain Points (Detailed)

### 1. Fragmented Information Sources
- Each of the 3,000+ US counties has its own website, format, and search interface
- No standardized way to search across counties
- Some counties are online, others require in-person visits or phone calls

### 2. Expertise Required
- Understanding lien priority (which liens survive foreclosure/tax deed)
- Knowing which records to search (Official Records, Court Records, Tax Collector, Code Enforcement)
- Interpreting legal documents (mortgage assignments, lis pendens, judgment abstracts)

### 3. Time Pressure
- Auction properties are typically listed 2-4 weeks before the auction date
- Investors researching multiple properties can spend 10-20 hours/week on research alone
- Missing a deadline means losing an opportunity

### 4. Risk of Missing Something
- A missed IRS lien can survive foreclosure and cost thousands
- An undetected HOA lien can accumulate daily
- Code violations can result in fines or demolition orders
- A pending lawsuit (lis pendens) can cloud title for years

### 5. Language Barrier (Brazilian Investors)
- County websites are English-only
- Legal terminology is difficult to translate
- Cultural differences in property law (Brazilian vs. US systems)
- Limited Portuguese-language resources about US real estate auctions
