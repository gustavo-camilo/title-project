# Marketing Strategy

## Brand Identity

### Name
**TitleSearch Pro** — Clear, professional, instantly communicates what the service does.

### Tagline
English: *"Know What You're Buying Before the Auction"*
Portuguese: *"Saiba o que voce esta comprando antes do leilao"*

### Visual Identity
- **Theme**: Dark mode primary (aligning with O Cacador de Titulos brand aesthetic)
- **Primary background**: Dark (#0a0a0a to #1a1a1a gradient)
- **Accent color**: Orange/gold (#f59e0b) — conveys trust, energy, premium feel
- **Secondary accent**: White (#ffffff) for headings, gray (#a1a1aa) for body text
- **Card surfaces**: Dark (#18181b) with subtle borders (#27272a)
- **Typography**: Inter for body text, bold geometric font for headings
- **Logo**: Shield or magnifying glass motif combined with a property silhouette

### Brand Voice
- **Professional but approachable** — not overly corporate, not too casual
- **Confident** — we know real estate research inside and out
- **Empowering** — helping customers make informed decisions
- **Bilingual** — naturally switching between English and Portuguese

## Value Proposition

### Primary Message
*"Stop spending hours searching county websites. Get a comprehensive title report for any US auction property — delivered in 24-48 hours."*

### Supporting Messages
1. **For newcomers**: "Don't risk buying a property with hidden liens. Our team researches everything so you don't have to."
2. **For experienced investors**: "Scale your auction business. Research 10 properties in the time it takes you to research 1."
3. **For Brazilian investors**: "O unico servico bilingue de pesquisa de titulos para investidores brasileiros no mercado americano."

## Social Media Strategy

### YouTube (Primary channel)
- **English channel**: "TitleSearch Pro"
- **Portuguese channel**: "TitleSearch Pro Brasil" (or bilingual with subtitles)
- **Content types**:
  - Educational tutorials (5-10 min): "How to Read a Title Report"
  - Case study walkthroughs: "We Found 3 Hidden Liens on This Property"
  - Quick tips (60-90 sec Shorts): "The #1 Mistake Auction Buyers Make"
  - Live Q&A sessions with title research experts
- **Frequency**: 1-2 videos/week

### Instagram / TikTok
- **Short-form content**: Reels/TikToks showing "lien horror stories"
- **Before/after**: Scattered county websites vs. clean TitleSearch Pro report
- **Stats and facts**: "Did you know 30% of auction properties have hidden liens?"
- **Frequency**: 3-5 posts/week

### Facebook
- **Page**: Company updates, blog post sharing, customer success stories
- **Groups**: Engage (don't spam) in REI groups, Brazilian investor groups
- **Ads**: See [Advertising Strategy](ADVERTISING_STRATEGY.md)

### LinkedIn
- **Company page**: B2B positioning for title companies and investment firms
- **Content**: Industry insights, partnership announcements
- **Frequency**: 1-2 posts/week

## Email Marketing

### Welcome Sequence (Automated)
1. **Day 0**: Welcome email + how TitleSearch Pro works
2. **Day 2**: Educational content (top blog post about auction risks)
3. **Day 5**: Social proof (customer testimonials, sample report preview)
4. **Day 7**: Offer — first credit at a discount or free sample report preview

### Ongoing Campaigns
- **Monthly newsletter**: Market updates, new blog posts, tips
- **Order follow-ups**: Report delivered → ask for feedback → upsell
- **Re-engagement**: Inactive customers after 60 days → reminder + offer
- **Credit expiration reminders**: 60 days, 30 days, 7 days before expiration

## Referral Program

### Mechanics
- **Give 1, Get 1**: When a customer refers someone who makes their first purchase, both parties receive 1 free credit
- **Unique referral link**: Each customer gets a shareable link from their dashboard
- **Tracking**: Referral attribution stored in the database
- **Limits**: Maximum 10 referral credits per customer per year (to prevent abuse)

### Promotion
- In-app banner on the dashboard
- Post-purchase email: "Share with a fellow investor, get a free search"
- Social sharing buttons (pre-filled message)

## Partnership Strategy

### Tier 1: Auction Education Companies
- **O Cacador de Titulos**: Cross-promotion to Brazilian investor audience
- **Other auction educators**: Affiliate partnerships — they promote us, we give their students a discount
- **Real estate investment coaches**: Bundle our service as part of their coaching packages

### Tier 2: Auction Platforms
- **RealAuction, Auction.com, Grant Street Group**: Integration or partnership for "one-click title search" from auction listings
- **County auction websites**: Featured service provider listing

### Tier 3: Professional Services
- **Real estate attorneys**: Refer clients who need preliminary research before legal consultation
- **Title companies**: White-label our research for their own clients
- **Real estate agents**: Provide title research for their auction-buying clients

## Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (2 weeks before)
1. Landing page live with email capture ("Get notified when we launch")
2. Tease on social media: "Something big is coming for auction investors"
3. Reach out to 10-20 potential beta customers for feedback
4. Prepare launch blog post and email

### Launch Week
1. Email launch announcement to captured leads
2. Social media campaign across all channels
3. Post in relevant Facebook groups and Reddit communities
4. Offer launch discount: 20% off all packages for the first week
5. Reach out to O Cacador de Titulos for cross-promotion

### Post-Launch (First 30 days)
1. Collect and showcase first customer testimonials
2. Start Google Ads campaign (see Advertising Strategy)
3. Publish first 3-4 blog posts
4. Engage daily in social media communities
5. Iterate based on customer feedback
